AI Engine

From AI that understands data to AI that changes outcomes

Connects your company’s document, process, equipment, quality, and supply chain data into a single knowledge structure. AI Engine goes beyond simply finding information or making predictions: it also presents the causes that influenced the results and the actions that need to change going forward.

When you have plenty of data but decisions are still hardAI Engine is a decision AI that understands and explains your company’s data and turns it into action.

What is AI Engine?

Why the result happened, and what needs to change

Companies already have plenty of data and AI tools. Yet on the ground, questions like these are still hard to answer:

Q1

Where in our documents are the regulations and the basis for decisions?

Q2

Why do defects and yield losses keep recurring?

Q3

How much will quality and productivity change if we adjust process conditions?

Q4

Out of all this data, which action would be most effective right now?

AI Engine connects scattered data into a structure that reflects how your company actually works, analyzes cause-and-effect relationships, and delivers actionable decisions.

If typical search and summarization AI

tells you “what is there,”

AI Engine also answers:

“Why did this result occur, and what effect can we expect from which action?”

The Principles of the AI Engine

AI Engine principles

01

Connects data in the language of your business

Documents, sensors, equipment logs, quality data, and work history are scattered in different formats. AI Engine structures them into the objects and relationships of real business operations, such as products, processes, equipment, materials, quality, and events.

02

Understands causes, beyond correlation

The correlation “there are more defects when the temperature is high” is not enough on its own. AI Engine analyzes the causal path through which a temperature change carries over to viscosity, thickness, and defect rate, and pinpoints where improvement is needed.

03

Turns analysis into actionable decisions

Analysis results do not stop at a dashboard. AI Engine proposes which conditions to adjust, what the expected effect is, and even the order in which to respond.

How does it work?

How AI Engine Factory works

  1. AI Engine Factory integrates MES, ERP, QMS, SCADA, equipment logs, sensor data, work orders, and inspection reports into a single ontology that links equipment, processes, products, lots, materials, operators, defects, inspection values, and environmental conditions.

  2. This makes it possible to trace which equipment, processes, materials, and operating conditions a specific defect or yield loss is linked to.

  3. By analyzing causal paths rather than simple correlations, it prioritizes the key factors that affect target KPIs such as defect rate, yield, and cycle time.

  4. It quantitatively estimates action effects, such as “How much will the defect rate drop if we lower the temperature?”, and also analyzes effects by condition, such as equipment, shift, and product group.

  5. It validates the reliability of results through sensitivity analysis and data support checks.

  6. What-if simulation compares outcomes before and after actions such as changing preventive maintenance intervals, adjusting process conditions, or replacing material lots.

  7. Based on the analysis, it proposes priority inspection targets, process conditions to adjust, and maintenance and quality responses, along with the evidence and causal paths behind them.

Key Features

Key features

01

Process ontology

Connects product–process–equipment–material–lot–operator–quality–maintenance data into a business structure. Common concepts can be reused across different plants, lines, and product groups, while site-specific characteristics can be extended through sub-ontologies.

02

Root cause analysis

Automatically explores the causal paths behind defects, yield loss, increased cycle time, equipment downtime, and more. Even in complex problems with many intertwined factors, it shows the main paths that influenced the outcome first.

03

Causal effect estimation

Estimates the effects of process conditions, equipment status, material properties, and work patterns on quality and production KPIs. Estimation methods suited to the nature of the problem can be used, including DML, difference-in-differences (DiD), g-formula, IPW, and regression discontinuity design (RDD).

04

What-if simulation

Predicts in advance the impact on quality and production of changes to process conditions, equipment status, or material properties. You can compare improvement directions and filter out high-risk conditions before actual line testing.

05

Improvement priority recommendations

Presents priority action items based on KPI impact, feasibility, and cost and other constraints. It goes beyond “What is the problem?” to suggest “What should we change first?”

06

Explainable analysis screens

Provides causal paths, supporting data, estimation results, reliability, and expected effects together. It is designed to make it easy for engineers and site managers to review AI results and use them in decision-making.
