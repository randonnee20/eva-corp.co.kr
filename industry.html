AREA 01

Process, quality & yield improvement AX

From the causes of yield loss to the impact of process conditions and raw materials, we analyze the causal relationships in manufacturing data to propose improvements that raise quality and productivity.

01Semiconductor Process Yield Improvement

Analyzes the relationships between yield and equipment, recipes, materials, environment, and work history to identify the key factors affecting yield and their contributions.

02Multivariate Process Condition Optimization

Analyzes how multiple process variables such as temperature, pressure, time, speed, and concentration affect quality, and recommends optimal conditions.

03Defect Propagation Tracing & Recurrence Prevention

Traces the paths by which a small anomaly in a specific process leads to downstream defects or customer claims, and identifies points for preemptive action.

04Raw Material Lot Impact Analysis

Analyzes the relationships between lot-level characteristics of raw materials and components, incoming inspection results, and storage and input history, and yield, reliability, and defect rates, to identify the key lots affecting quality and the contribution of each factor.

AREA 02

Materials, supply chain & inventory AX

From supply chain risks to raw material supply and inventory operations, we analyze the causal relationships among key factors to reduce production disruptions and support stable supply and efficient inventory operations.

01Supply Chain Risk Early Warning

Links suppliers, raw materials, lead times, geopolitical risks, quality history, and production plans to detect potential supply disruptions early.

02Raw Material Supply & Inventory Optimization

An AI that analyzes the relationships among demand changes, order quantities, safety stock, delivery, and price fluctuations to reduce both excess inventory and material shortages at the same time.

03Integrated Inventory, Production & Delivery Decisions

Calculates optimal plans across the entire value chain so that decisions to cut inventory do not lead to production stoppages or delivery delays.

AREA 01

Corporate credit policy & lending strategy optimization AX

From credit assessment and loan approval to interest rate and credit limit setting and delinquency management, we analyze the causal relationships among key factors to propose lending strategies that balance profit and risk.

01Credit Assessment & Loan Approval

Links corporate clients’ financial, credit, transaction, industry, and market data to analyze repayment capacity and default risk, supporting approve, decline, and conditional approval decisions.

02Loan Interest Rate & Credit Limit Optimization

Analyzes how loan interest rates, credit limits, repayment terms, and collateral conditions affect approval rates, delinquency rates, and profitability, and proposes optimal terms by customer and portfolio.

03Delinquency Prevention & Debt Restructuring

Analyzes repayment history, cash flow, industry risk, and transaction patterns to identify the likelihood and causes of delinquency, and proposes effective prevention and restructuring strategies for each customer.

04Collections Strategy Optimization

Analyzes customer circumstances, delinquency stage, past response history, and channel response to propose collections strategies that weigh recovery likelihood against cost.

AREA 02

Financial product pricing & margin optimization AX

From loan interest rates and premiums to promotions and fees, we analyze how pricing policies causally affect customer behavior and profit, and propose optimal plans that balance risk, competitiveness, and long-term profitability.

01Loan Interest Rates & Risk-Based Pricing

Analyzes customer creditworthiness, collateral, transaction history, market interest rates, and competitive conditions to propose loan interest rate strategies that balance risk and profitability.

02Premium & Renewal Optimization

Analyzes customer risk, insurance product characteristics, renewal history, and the competitive environment to determine how premium levels and renewal strategies affect retention, loss ratios, and profitability.

03Customer Lifetime Value & Margin Optimization

Analyzes customers’ transaction history, product holdings, channel usage, churn likelihood, and cross-sell response to propose strategies that raise both customer lifetime value and margins.

04Promotion & Fee Effects

Analyzes the relationships between promotion type, target customers, fee structure, channel, and timing, and revenue, new customer acquisition, churn, and margins, to propose profitable promotion strategies.
